A dramatic, extreme low-angle perspective looking directly up into the hollow interior of an incandescent lightbulb. The smooth, sweeping black outline of the glass bulb vaults overhead like an expansive dome against a solid white background. Inside, perched atop the bulb's screw base, rests a sprawling miniature city rendered in crisp, flat geometric line work. Slender rectangular skyscrapers, sharp triangular roofs, and stylized spherical treetops populate the interior landscape. The central filament arches high across the composition, cleverly doubled as an elegant, sweeping suspension bridge with thin vertical cables connecting to tiny roadway platforms. Every element is constructed with deliberate, uniform black vector outlines, featuring bold silhouettes, clean semicircular arcs, and precise straight lines. The entire composition remains strictly two-dimensional, graphic, and starkly minimal, completely void of fills, shading, cross-hatching, gradients, or tone.
