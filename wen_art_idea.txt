A side-profile cross-section of an oversized vintage mechanical alarm clock, rendered entirely in bold, razor-sharp black vector outlines on a pure white background. The clock's exterior features stark, perfect geometric curves, twin top bells, and minimalist peg legs. 

Inside the hollow body, the internal mechanism transforms into a tiered, miniature mountain landscape. Towering gear wheels double as sheer, stepped cliffs, their crisp rectangular teeth forming terraced plateaus. Between these mechanical peaks, a tiny suspension bridge drawn with single, precise lines spans a vast interior chasm. Microscopic geometric houses with triangular roofs rest along the perimeter of the giant mainspring. A minimalist railway track emerges from a circular aperture in the clock's side, curving gracefully through empty space and looping around the central winding spindle. 

The composition utilizes high contrast, flat two-dimensional geometry, and clever perspective play, portraying an immense, quiet world nestled inside an everyday machine using only pure, unshaded black contour lines.
