A crisp, minimalist 2D line-art illustration of an impossible machine engineered to capture and fold intangible horizons into flat geometric sheets. Viewed from a dramatic, steep diagonal perspective, the structure slices diagonally across the canvas from the upper left to the lower right.

The apparatus centers around a massive, tapering polygonal intake funnel composed of concentric hexagonal rims. Extending from its narrow base is an intricate mechanical loom with razor-sharp scissor arms, interlocking circular gears, and cantilevered guide needles. An unbroken horizon line feeds directly into the wide mouth, passing through a sequence of faceted diamond-shaped rollers that crease the intangible line into perfectly nested, accordion-folded polygons stacked upon an angled exit rail. Slender, arcing conduits and calibrated caliper-like joints branch outward, establishing a bold, imaginative silhouette.

Rendered entirely with deliberate, sharp black vector outlines on an empty, pure white background, the design remains strictly flat and graphic, featuring no color, gray tones, shading, shadows, gradients, hatching, textures, or text.
