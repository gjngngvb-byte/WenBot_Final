An extreme, straight-down bird’s-eye view peering directly into the hollowed circular interior of an opened pocket watch, its glass face shattered and removed. 

Instead of brass cogs, the watch’s shallow casing contains a sprawling, brutally organized poultry stockyard rendered in ultra-fine, microscopic crosshatching. Narrow concentric walkways and radial iron pens divide the space into hundreds of geometric sectors, strictly mirroring the missing clockwork. Thousands of wingless, oversized white chickens are corralled inside, clustered tightly around tiny industrial feed troughs that double as roman numerals. 

In the dead center, where the hands once pivoted, stands a miniature elevated guard tower made of welded sewing needles. A solitary, faceless watchmaker sits atop it on a swivel stool, clutching an ink-stained ledger and peering downward through a jeweler’s loupe at the livestock. 

The composition is stark and graphic: pure white paper contrast against dense black ink stippling. The extreme top-down perspective flattens the scene, locking the rigid, claustrophobic agriculture inside the perfect metallic circle of the pocket watch’s polished silver rim.
