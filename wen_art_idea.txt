A dramatic low-angle 2D vector illustration of a standard office binder clip behaving like a stalking, predatory insect. Viewed from directly underneath, the object looms massively above the viewer. The heavy triangular steel body arches high like an armored carapace, its flared base tilted downward to suggest a powerful, open-jawed head preparing to strike. The two looped wire handles are splayed and bent forward into long, articulated forelegs with crisp, mechanical elbows that terminate firmly against the ground plane.

The artwork features precise, bold black outlines set against a pure, stark white background. Clean, deliberate curves delineate the metallic tension of the wire legs, contrasted with the sharp, geometric geometry of the folded steel body. There are no gradients, no gray tones, no shadows, no hatching, and no textures. The design remains completely flat, minimal, and graphic, relying entirely on continuous line weight and striking spatial perspective to turn a mundane stationery item into a formidable mechanical beast.
