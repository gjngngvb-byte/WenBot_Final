An extreme worm’s-eye view looking straight up from the ground into a dreamlike metropolis. Framing the edges, sleek, minimalist skyscrapers rendered in bold, crisp black outlines taper sharply inward toward the zenith. The towers are drawn with pure geometric discipline: sharp vertical lines, flat rectangular planes, and sparse arrays of tiny square window frames. The physically impossible element dominates the center: instead of piercing the open sky, the tops of the converging towers smoothly bend and fuse together, weaving into a single, flawless, impossible continuous geometric knot suspended overhead. The linework is entirely flat and vector-sharp, consisting exclusively of clean black curves and straight edges on a stark, pure white background. There is no shading, hatching, texture, or gray values, using only the structural convergence of the outlines to create an imaginative, highly readable silhouette that balances dramatic perspective with flat, graphic elegance.
