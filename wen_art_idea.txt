A steep diagonal composition plunging from upper-left to lower-right, showcasing a surreal machine designed to harvest and unspool intangible echoes. Rendered entirely in razor-sharp, uniform black vector outlines against an immaculate white background, the apparatus is completely flat, graphic, and visually clear.

At the top, a flaring geometric intake cone with tiered concentric rims channels empty space downward along three parallel, sweeping curved conduits. The conduits route through a cluster of hollow, toothless orbital gears and cantilevered balance needles suspended on delicate fulcrums. In the middle, the structure bridges across an open aperture ring framed by pristine triangular brackets. Below, an elongated focal nozzle tapers into a precision needle, feeding the extracted nonexistence onto a minimal rotating spool rendered as interlocking nested circles.

Every component uses bold, deliberate line work and pure geometric simplicity, establishing an intriguing silhouette that tilts dynamically through the frame. The design relies entirely on pristine single-weight outlines and negative space, void of any fill, tone, hatching, or dimension, capturing a clever, impossible mechanism with stark graphic elegance.
