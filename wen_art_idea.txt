An extreme bird’s-eye view looking straight down into the flat cross-section of a halved apple. The entire composition is rendered in crisp, uniform black vector outlines on a solid white background. 

The outermost silhouette is a smooth, minimal circle with a tiny circular notch indicating the stem axis. In the center, where the core normally rests, five symmetrical seed chambers fan out in a precise pentagonal star. Each chamber reveals a different miniature geometric environment: one cavity contains a tiny village of sharp-angled rooftops; another features three stylized triangular pine trees; the third encloses a clean, spiraling labyrinth path; the fourth holds a solitary geometric lighthouse with radiating line beams; and the fifth shows a miniature sailboat resting on three simple wave lines. 

The entire design relies on pure white negative space, deliberate monoline curves, and flat geometric clarity, creating an imaginative hidden world with zero shading, gradients, or textures.
