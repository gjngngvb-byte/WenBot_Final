An extreme worm’s-eye perspective looking directly upward from street level into a dreamlike metropolis. Towering, ultra-minimalist skyscrapers with crisp geometric contours surge vertically from the edges of the frame, tapering dramatically toward the center. Sharp rectilinear facades feature sparse, pristine rectangular window outlines rendered purely in deliberate black vector-style line art. Defying all physics, the massive architectural monoliths curve inward like pliable ribbons at their peaks, seamlessly braiding together into an intricate, perfect geometric knot suspended in the open sky. From the top of this central knot, a single razor-sharp spire extends straight into the void. The entire composition is constructed entirely of uniform, clean black line work on a pure, stark white background. With no shading, gradients, hatching, or textures, the piece relies strictly on clean perspective convergence, flat two-dimensional geometry, and clever negative space to convey an impossible, graphic cityscape.
