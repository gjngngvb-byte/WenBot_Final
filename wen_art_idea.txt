A top-down 2D vector illustration of an enigmatic alien navigational artifact, centered on a pure white background. Drawn entirely with crisp, deliberate black outlines, without fills, shading, or gradients. The artifact features a central circular core enclosed within three concentric, segmented rings that interlock like a precise celestial mechanism. Extending outward from the core are four slender, geometric pylons arranged in a cardinal cross, each terminating in a sharp teardrop aperture. Nestled between the pylons are elegant, sweeping crescent fins with uniform circular cutouts of diminishing sizes. Razor-sharp geometric notches interrupt the outer perimeter, giving the silhouette a cryptic, advanced logic reminiscent of a technological glyph. The line work is completely flat, clean, and balanced, relying entirely on bold graphic shapes and negative space for maximum visual impact.
