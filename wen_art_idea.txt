A graphic 2D illustration composed exclusively of crisp, uniform black vector outlines on a stark white background. The composition captures an extreme macro close-up looking steeply upward from an exaggerated perspective. Dominating the immediate foreground is the colossal, curving rim of an open clam shell, rendered in clean, confident geometric arcs. Nestled inside, in place of a pearl, sits an impossible miniature campfire constructed of perfectly smoothed coral logs, topped with clean, stylized flame ribbons. Floating directly above the flames is a colossal, razor-sharp circular air bubble; inside this bubble, a single tiny sea turtle swims upward through inverted space. The dramatic depth-of-field effect is conveyed purely through line scale and perspective, with gigantic, sweeping curves framing the lower edge and minute, precise figures receding into the upper negative space. The entire design relies on pure silhouette, clever conceptual juxtaposition, flat geometry, and pristine line weight, entirely free of color, gray tones, shading, shadows, textures, or hatching.
