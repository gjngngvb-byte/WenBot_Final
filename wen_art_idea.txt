A dramatic, worm’s-eye perspective looking upward through a canyon of towering, minimalist skyscrapers. Sharp, crisp black vector outlines define the flat, geometric silhouettes of the buildings, which converge steeply toward the upper sky against a stark, pure white background. The building facades are reduced to precise, rhythmic rectangular grids and clean vertical structural lines. Defying gravity, a sleek two-lane highway peels away from the lower street and twists upward in an impossible, continuous ribbon-like loop between the towers, curving smoothly into an inverted loop-de-loop high overhead. Along this vertical Möbius-like road, simplified geometric silhouettes of cars travel upside down without falling. Slender pedestrian skybridges connect the summits at impossible angles, intersecting without supports. The entire composition is rendered exclusively in unshaded, razor-sharp, uniform black outlines, creating a striking, graphic, and dreamlike urban paradox with completely flat white negative space and zero tone, texture, or hatching.
