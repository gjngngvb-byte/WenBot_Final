An extreme bird’s-eye view looking directly down upon a mysterious crouching figure constructed entirely from nested, curling ribbons of flexible acetate sheet. At the dead center rests a stark, perfect circle representing the apex of a hood, inset with two sharp, minimalist crescent shapes indicating an upturned face. Radiating outward from this central nexus are sweeping, concentric curved bands that interlock and fan out in a stylized, radial composition, forming the overlapping drapery of a sweeping mantle and folded limbs. Every curved ribbon terminates in clean, deliberate right angles, producing a clever, labyrinthine silhouette that reads as both an intricate geometric mandala and an enigmatic humanoid form. Rendered strictly with uniform, ultra-clean black vector linework on a solid, untextured pure white background. The entire design is entirely flat and two-dimensional, with zero color, gray, shading, shadows, gradients, textures, or hatching.
