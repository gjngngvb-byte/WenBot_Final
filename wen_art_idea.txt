An extreme worm's-eye view looking straight up from street level into a dreamlike geometric metropolis. Monolithic skyscrapers rise along the outer edges in steep, dramatic perspective. Defying physics, the tops of these towering buildings bend inward and seamlessly weave together, looping over and under one another to form a single, impossible continuous knot suspended directly overhead. 

Through the central opening of the architectural knot, a tiny, pristine crescent moon hovers in the empty zenith. The buildings feature sparse, perfectly aligned square and rectangular window cutouts that cleanly conform to the warping curves of the facades. 

The composition is rendered strictly as flat 2D digital line art, using razor-sharp, deliberate black vector outlines against an immaculate, pure white background. There are no colors, gray tones, shading, shadows, gradients, hatching, or textures, relying entirely on bold silhouettes, clever geometry, and dynamic perspective to convey the impossible surreal landscape.
