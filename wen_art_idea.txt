A bizarre hybrid organism resembling an oversized, geometric carnivorous bloom, viewed from a dramatically foreshortened three-quarter angle. The massive front-facing flower head thrusts forward toward the viewer, defined by nested, sharply curved petals that overlap like interlocking mechanical camera aperture blades. At the center of the blossom, a smooth cylindrical pistil emerges, encircled by concentric ring gears and tiny recessed rivet circles. Extending backwards into extreme perspective, the organism’s stem consists of segmented, telescoping cylindrical joints connected by clean, exposed ball-and-socket nodes. Rigid, geometric leaves branch outward, stylized as folding solar-panel facets with smooth, rounded corners. Long, curling tendrils made of flexible ribbed conduit loop gracefully through the composition, balancing the silhouette. The entire artwork consists exclusively of precise, uniform black vector outlines set against a stark, solid white background, with completely flat interior spaces, strictly devoid of any color, gray tones, shading, shadows, hatching, or texture.
