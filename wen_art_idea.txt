A macro, exaggerated low-angle perspective looking directly into the sliced cross-section of a hollowed-out incandescent lightbulb. The bulb's exterior contour is defined by bold, crisp black geometric curves resting on a minimal threaded screw base. Inside the hollow glass dome, the tungsten filament transforms into a soaring, minimalist suspension bridge spanning across sharply defined geometric mountain ridges. Tiny stylized train tracks curve along the bridge, disappearing into a minuscule tunnel bored through an interior peak. Simple concentric line-rings evoke miniature orbiting celestial paths above the bridge. The entire composition utilizes pure, razor-sharp vector line art on an immaculate, solid white field. Line weights vary deliberately to establish dramatic scale and perspective, with thick, punchy foreground contours on the bulb's outer rim tapering to ultra-fine, precise interior strokes. Every element is flat, graphic, and strictly unshaded, creating a clever and surreal micro-universe.
