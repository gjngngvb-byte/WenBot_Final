An extreme worm’s-eye perspective looking straight up from the floor of an open, hollowed pocket watch. Towering vertical cogs, balance wheels, and escapement levers rise like monumental art-deco skyscrapers, their crisp gear teeth forming jagged, geometric rooflines that recede sharply toward the sky. Slender arched bridges and taught coiled springs stretch between the metal towers like elevated pedestrian walkways and suspension cables. At the highest point, the circular rim of the watch casing frames the view like an immense celestial dome, with the sweeping silhouettes of an hour and minute hand looming overhead like gigantic planetary rings. 

Rendered entirely in sharp, deliberate black vector line art on a pure white background. The composition relies solely on bold contours, pristine circular arcs, and precise geometric forms, completely flat with no fills, shading, hatching, gradients, or textures.
