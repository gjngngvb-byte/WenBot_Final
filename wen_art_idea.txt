An extreme worm's-eye view looking directly upward into the underside of a colossal, hovering alien atmospheric probe. The central core consists of nested, concentric geometric rings tapering into a hollow, sharp hexagonal aperture at the apex. Radiating outward are three sweeping, sickle-shaped pylons that curve dramatically downward toward the viewer, ending in crisp, tapered points. Between the primary pylons, delicate geometric trusses and ultra-fine needle-like antennae form a symmetrical, graphic lattice resembling an intricate navigational compass. The entire composition dominates the frame, creating a striking graphic mandala overhead. The artwork is rendered exclusively in crisp, uniform black vector-like line art on a stark, pure white background. All planes are flat and unshaded, using only clean geometric outlines, elegant curves, and high-contrast negative space to define the alien structure’s immense scale and upward perspective.
