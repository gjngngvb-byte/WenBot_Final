An extreme macro, low-angle 2D vector illustration of a cracked walnut shell split wide open, revealing a hidden geometric civilization inside. Bold, smooth, uniform black outlines define the outer contours of the shell against a pure white background. Inside the curved cavern of the shell, a miniature stepped city emerges with clean architectural silhouettes: crisp rectangular towers, precise semi-circular archways, zig-zagging staircases, and delicate suspension bridges spanning the central divide. Suspended near the top opening, a tiny geometric hot air balloon floats between the two halves. The composition features an exaggerated sense of scale, using sharp perspective lines and deliberate contour weights to convey immense depth within a tiny vessel. The artwork is completely flat, graphic, and minimal, rendered exclusively in pristine black monoline work on stark white.
