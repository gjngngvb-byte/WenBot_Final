A dramatic top-down high-angle view looking directly into an impossible, surreal architectural tower. The structure is formed from a continuous, looping geometric ribbon that twists into an inverted hexagonal spiral, descending endlessly into a central void like an Escherian paradox. Each tier is composed of flat, cantilevered balconies that hover without support, punctuated by perfectly simplified semicircular arches and clean rectangular window cutouts that connect impossibly between different elevations. Flat, uniform staircases curve along sharp polygonal edges, defying gravity by seamlessly morphing into vertical pillars. The composition is executed entirely in crisp, deliberate black monoline outlines against a solid, pristine white background. The forms rely solely on balanced geometric shapes, smooth clean curves, and precise straight vectors to define the impossible perspective. The design remains resolutely graphic and two-dimensional, with no color, gray fills, shading, shadows, gradients, hatching, or surface textures.
