The abdomen is a long, streamlined cylinder featuring minimalist circuit channels and micro-actuators, supported by ultra-thin, geometric spindly legs perched upon a clean horizontal bar. Its folded wings are defined solely through intersecting polygonal outlines and crisp geometric facets. The entire artwork consists strictly of uniform, pitch-black outlines set against a pure white void, relying exclusively on bold silhouette interplay and flat, overlapping geometric shapes to convey perspective without any fills, gray tones, shading, hatching, or gradients.
